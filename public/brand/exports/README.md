## Required contrast rule

The upper chevron is deep dark navy on the cream tile and cream on the navy tile; it is
10% smaller than the original. The lower chevron must not appear. The
same crooked-smile robot character appears in both versions. The cream-tile
chevron and robot use the darkest core navy from the alternate tile—not the
former medium blue; the
navy-tile chevron and robot retain the original cream scheme. Use the
cream-tile primary on light or mixed surfaces and the navy-tile alternate on
dark surfaces.

Do not recolor, stretch, rotate, crop through, or rearrange any export.
