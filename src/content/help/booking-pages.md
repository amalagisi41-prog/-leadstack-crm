Create a buyer-consultation or listing-appointment booking page, then verify that a client can choose a slot and receive a confirmation.

Lead forms and booking pages are **Live**. Start with one clearly named appointment type that fits your actual schedule. A booking page only helps if the displayed timezone, working hours, hosts, and next steps match how you meet clients. Test the complete flow before placing the link in an open-house follow-up message or on your public website.

## Before you start

Choose the meeting’s purpose, duration, location or meeting URL, and the person who will host it. Allow travel time between showings and appointments. Decide what you need to know before the consultation, such as whether the prospect is buying or selling. Keep intake short enough that a person on a phone can finish it comfortably.

## Numbered setup steps

1. Open the workspace’s booking pages and create a page. The exact sidebar wording is **[VERIFY IN APP]** for your workspace navigation. In Basics, enter Page name, URL slug, and Description. Use a specific name such as Buyer consultation so the client knows what they are booking. Check the slug before sharing the link; do not assume a future rename will preserve every previously shared URL.
2. Add Meeting URL if this appointment uses a video meeting link, then review Status. Start with a draft while you configure the page. Avoid placing a private staff meeting link in a buyer-facing appointment by mistake. If the appointment is in person, explain the arrangement clearly in the description and confirmation rather than promising an unverified automatic address lookup.
3. Configure Slot rules: Duration, Buffer between meetings, Timezone, Visible days, Min notice (hours), and Max per day. Set a buffer that allows you to finish notes and travel after a showing. Check the timezone explicitly. A calendar that looks open to you can produce confusing client options when the booking page uses a different timezone.
4. Select Hosts / team and review the working hours available for those hosts. Confirm that the intended agent is eligible and available. Booking logic checks slots again when a reservation is submitted, so an old browser tab may not retain a slot someone else has just taken. Do not tell a buyer the appointment is confirmed until the reservation actually succeeds.
5. Review Intake form and add only the information needed to prepare. The booking submission requires name, email, and phone, with additional fields governed by the page’s intake rules. Try the form on a phone. A buyer consultation may need different questions from a listing appointment, so use a separate page when the meeting purpose or preparation differs.
6. Complete Confirmation + reminders. Review Confirmation message, optional Redirect URL, and the available reminder options. Tell the client what to expect next and what to prepare. Use a redirect only when the destination is ready and relevant. Reminder delivery depends on the configured services; selecting an option is not a substitute for testing the actual message.
7. Review optional Payment, Territory, and Visual sections if they appear. A free buyer introduction should not accidentally request a deposit. Payment settings use a separate configured payment connection; a payment-required booking can remain awaiting payment rather than confirmed. Review the displayed amount and hold window if you deliberately use payment. Territory and visual options should match the intended workspace and public identity.
8. Save the page, set the intended public status, and open its public link. The exact save button wording is **[VERIFY IN APP]**. Make a controlled test reservation with contact details you own. Check the available hours, intake questions, success screen, confirmation email, and calendar entry. For an unpaid consultation, verify that the resulting booking is scheduled before sharing the page broadly.

## Common mistakes

The frequent problems are the wrong timezone, no host availability, insufficient travel buffers, and publishing before checking the confirmation. Another is treating an awaiting-payment hold as a confirmed consultation. A successfully saved editor form is only a configuration check; the public booking and resulting appointment are the real end-to-end check. Repeat that check after changing host hours or slot rules.

Share the tested link with the right audience: buyer consultation after an open house, listing appointment after a seller inquiry, or a short catch-up for your sphere. Monitor early reservations and keep a clear next task for each prospect. Use the appointment to prepare a useful conversation, then move the related deal forward based on what actually happened.
