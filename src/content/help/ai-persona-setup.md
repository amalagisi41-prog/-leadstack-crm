Set up and test a real estate AI persona that uses your business information and gives clients a clear path to reach you.

AI Web Chat and AI Assist are **Private Preview**. SMS and voice automation are also **Private Preview** and require separate channel setup. Saving a persona does not activate every channel or guarantee that a prospect receives a response. Review the instructions as carefully as you would review a listing description sent under your name.

## Before you start

Write down your brokerage name, service area, business hours, preferred buyer-consultation process, and human escalation contact. Use the same facts you publish elsewhere. The Business Blueprint supplies business context, but a generated draft still needs your review. Keep confidential client facts out of a general persona; it describes your practice, not an individual transaction.

## Numbered setup steps

1. Open your workspace’s Business Blueprint or business profile and review the business information you have already saved. Correct outdated brokerage details, neighborhoods, and services before relying on generated instructions. The precise location of any persona-generation button in that flow is **[VERIFY IN APP]**. Do not assume that changing a public website also updates a saved profile.
2. Open the AI agent profile for the same workspace. The form includes Persona / system prompt, Business name, an escalation notification email, Active from, Until, Timezone, and escalation keywords. Confirm that you are editing the intended agent workspace before making changes, particularly when supporting a team member’s setup.
3. Read Persona / system prompt from beginning to end. Describe how you want buyer inquiries, listing appointment requests, and past-client questions handled. Make the next action concrete: gather the requested information, offer an appropriate consultation link, or hand the conversation to you. Avoid claims about property availability, financing, or transaction outcomes that the system cannot verify.
4. Review Business name and the escalation notification email. Use an inbox someone actually monitors while you are in showings. If your brokerage has approved wording or instructions for handoff, reflect that in the prompt. Do not assume that naming a colleague in the persona automatically gives them workspace access or forwards a message to their phone.
5. Set Active from, Until, and Timezone for the practice you actually run. A Connecticut agent should double-check the chosen timezone rather than trusting a browser default. Think through evenings and weekends when open-house inquiries arrive. The exact behavior of each channel outside these hours is **[VERIFY IN APP]** and should be tested separately before launch.
6. Review escalation keywords. The screen supports a keyword list, with examples including manager, human, complaint, and stop ai. Choose understandable terms and test them in context. Do not treat escalation keywords as a substitute for a channel’s consent or opt-out handling. A request to speak to you needs a practical follow-through process after the system identifies it.
7. Click Save profile and wait for confirmation. Reopen or reload the profile to check that the intended prompt and settings persisted. If saving fails, resolve the error before continuing. Keep a copy of approved wording in your own operating notes so you can explain what changed when reviewing unexpected answers later.
8. Use the available test conversation area to try realistic scenarios: a new buyer asking for a showing, a seller requesting a listing consultation, and a past client asking for a vendor recommendation. Include a direct request for a human. Check factual accuracy and the proposed next action, then revise and save again. Exact channel-specific sandbox controls are **[VERIFY IN APP]**.

## Common mistakes

A long prompt cannot compensate for incorrect business facts. Another common error is assuming that saving profile hours activates SMS, voice, and web chat together. Review each channel independently. Never describe a test conversation as proof of live delivery: a sandbox answer and a delivered client message are different checks. Start with a controlled test recipient before a live buyer workflow.

Review the persona after a brokerage change, a service-area change, or a change in who handles escalations. Keep the tone clear and professional. Measure the time from a test inquiry to the actual response only after the intended channel is connected and ready; under 60 seconds is the workflow goal, not a promise established by saving this form.
