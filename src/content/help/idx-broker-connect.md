Connect your own IDX Broker account to the correct real estate workspace and check the listing experience before sharing it with buyers.

IDX listings are **Private Preview**. You need your own active IDX Broker account and the MLS permissions appropriate to your market. AgentStack uses a workspace-specific connection; it does not grant MLS access or supply a shared brokerage feed. Confirm preview access before planning a launch around listing search. Keep your existing buyer-facing search available until you have tested the replacement.

## Before you start

Find the access key for the IDX Broker account authorized for your practice. The precise location of that key in the provider’s dashboard is **[VERIFY IN APP]**. Do not send the key to a buyer or paste it into a public page. If you work across more than one MLS, identify which approved feed belongs in this workspace. Check the workspace name before connecting anything.

## Numbered connection steps

1. Open the workspace’s IDX settings. Navigation can depend on enabled features; the exact sidebar wording is **[VERIFY IN APP]**. If the connection screen is unavailable, check preview access with the workspace owner. Do not use another agent’s credentials simply to make the settings screen appear populated.
2. Locate the IDX Broker access key field and paste the authorized key. The screen does not ask for an IDX Broker password. Remove accidental spaces introduced by copying, and verify that you selected the intended provider account. Keep the key in its dedicated field rather than Business Blueprint notes or lead-facing copy.
3. Review MLS id (optional). Leave it blank to use your account’s default approved MLS, as the screen explains. If the account is approved for multiple feeds, enter the appropriate approved MLS ID. Do not substitute a listing number, your agent license number, or an office identifier for the feed ID.
4. Click Connect IDX Broker and wait for the response. If the connection fails, preserve the error message and recheck the key and approved feed. Do not repeatedly replace the key with unrelated accounts. A successful save is the start of verification; it is not proof that every photo, listing status, or public inquiry works correctly.
5. Review the connected state. The screen includes an MLS ID control for an existing connection and can load approved MLS feeds. If you change the selected feed, save it and repeat the listing checks. A feed change can affect the inventory buyers see, so make it deliberately rather than while troubleshooting an unrelated display issue.
6. Open the workspace listing view and inspect properties you recognize in your market. Compare address, price, status, photos, and attribution with the authorized source. Check an active property and another status if available. The exact refresh timing and any provider-side sync controls are **[VERIFY IN APP]**; do not advertise an unverified update interval.
7. Review the public search experience associated with the workspace. Check it on a phone as well as your desktop. Verify that the brokerage identity and required listing notices appear correctly. Connecting a feed does not automatically authorize a domain change or replace your current website. The exact public-site publishing action is **[VERIFY IN APP]** for your setup.
8. Submit one clearly identified test listing inquiry using contact details you control. Check that it reaches the intended workspace and that the property context makes sense. Follow the contact through your normal next action, such as a buyer-consultation invitation. If follow-up uses SMS or AI, those channels require their own Private Preview configuration and testing.

## Common mistakes

The most disruptive mistake is connecting the wrong account to a workspace because the agent administers several brands. Another is entering an MLS ID the provider account cannot access. Treat these as permission and configuration questions, not problems that can be fixed by copying a different agent’s feed. If the account is connected but listings look wrong, investigate the selected market and feed before changing public pages.

Keep the access key private, and verify any brokerage or MLS display requirements through the appropriate authorized source. A listing inquiry that creates a contact still needs an agent-owned next action. Use a small set of test properties and one test inquiry as your launch checklist, then recheck them after an account or feed change.
